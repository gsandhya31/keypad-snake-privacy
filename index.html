Privacy Policy

Keypad Snake

Effective Date: October 2026  |  App Version: 1.0.0

Thank you for playing Keypad Snake ("the App"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it through this Privacy Policy.

Summary of Our Privacy Practices

  • No Personal Accounts: You do not need to create an account or sign in.
  • No Advertisements: There are no third-party ads or trackers.
  • No Advertising ID: We have explicitly disabled and removed Android Advertising ID collection (AD_ID).
  • No Data Selling or Sharing: We do not sell, rent, or trade your data with any third parties.
  • Offline-First: Gameplay, sound effects, and high scores function entirely locally on your device.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects only minimal, pseudonymous usage data via Google Firebase Analytics to help us understand gameplay engagement and fix potential issues:

  • App Interactions & Events: Anonymous counters when game events occur (such as boot_completed, menu_opened, snake_selected, game_started, game_over with final score and duration in seconds, game_restarted, and new high_score).
  • Device / Instance Identifiers: A randomly generated pseudonymous App Instance ID provided by Firebase to differentiate unique app installations and compute basic aggregate metrics (such as daily active users and retention).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We believe in absolute data minimization:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location (GPS or coarse).
  • We do not collect or request the Android Advertising Identifier (Google Play advertising ID is explicitly disabled via manifest configuration).
  • We do not collect financial, credit card, or payment information.
  • We do not perform device fingerprinting (no IMEI, MAC address, or hardware serial numbers).

3. Local Storage & Backups

Your high score and audio/vibration preferences are stored strictly locally on your device using Android SharedPreferences. If you have Android Auto-Backup enabled on your device, Android may securely back up your personal best high score to your private Google Drive account as part of the operating system's standard backup service.

4. Third-Party Service Providers

We use Google Firebase Analytics (provided by Google LLC) for anonymous usage measurement. All data transmitted to Google is encrypted in transit using HTTPS. For more information on how Google processes and protects analytics data, please consult:

Google Privacy Policy  |  Firebase Privacy & Security Information

5. Children's Privacy

Keypad Snake is rated for adults and nostalgic mobile gamers (target audience 18+). The App does not knowingly collect any personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Aggregated, pseudonymous analytics data collected through Firebase Analytics is retained in accordance with standard Firebase retention periods (up to 14 months) and then automatically expunged. Since the App does not collect user accounts or identifying personal information, there is no personal profile to delete. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored game scores and preferences.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published directly on this page with an updated effective date.
